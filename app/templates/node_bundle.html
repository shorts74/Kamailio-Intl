What this is
The Manager's own authoritative copy of the node-side kamailio.cfg.template and scripts. Every node's Apply & Restart pushes exactly what's stored here before syncing and restarting -- this is what makes a restart apply the latest code, not just the latest data. Without an upload here, Apply & Restart only ever refreshes data and re-runs whatever's already on each node's disk.
